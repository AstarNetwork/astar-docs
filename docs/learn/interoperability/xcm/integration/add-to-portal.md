# Add assets to Astar Portal

Once a channel is opened between parachains and assets have been registered, you can request to have your assets added to the [Astar Portal](https://portal.astar.network).

Share your parachain and asset details with the Astar team:

- Reach out via the [official Astar Discord](https://discord.gg/astarnetwork)
- Or use the [Astar Contact Form](https://astar.network/contact)
